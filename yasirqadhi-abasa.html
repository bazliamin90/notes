“He Frowned and Turned Away” — Sūrah ʿAbasa [80:1–10]

Why there is nothing awkward about this story, what the Prophet ﷺ actually wanted, and the lessons it holds: gentle correction, the heart over the body, certain over uncertain gain, conveying rather than converting, and the life of Ibn Umm Maktūm.
