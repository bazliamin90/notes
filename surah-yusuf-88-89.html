"Did You Know What You Did to Yusuf and His Brother?" [Ayat 88–89]

The brothers stand before the Aziz with worthless goods and a plea for charity — every phrase an unintended irony — and Yusuf (AS) finally answers with a single, piercing question. Includes a follow-up on why Yaʿqūb (AS) said "Yusuf and his brother" in Ayah 87.
