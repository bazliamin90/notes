“He Frowned and Turned Away” — Sūrah ʿAbasa

A rebuke to the Prophet ﷺ over a frown at a blind man becomes a study in the sins of the prophets, the ethics of calling to Allah, how to treat the Qurʾān and those who carry it, and Allah’s proofs of resurrection — from the origin of man, to the food he eats, to the Day when everyone flees from everyone.
Ayāt 1–42 ʿIṣmah of the Prophets Ethics of Daʿwah Resurrection Intercession
