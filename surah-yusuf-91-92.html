"Allah Has Preferred You Over Us" [Ayat 91–92]

The brothers' shaken confession and Yusuf's carefully worded reply — a study in the oath "Ta-Allahi", the hidden word athara, the gravity of khāṭiʾīn, the anatomy of tathrīb, and a realistic, non-simplistic view of forgiveness.
