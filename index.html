These pages are put together by Bazli Aminuddin, a student of the Qur'an — not a scholar, and not someone claiming any authority in tafsīr. This is simply a personal effort to take notes seriously and keep them organized in one place.

A note on what this is — and isn't

Everything on this site is a compilation of notes from classes, lectures, and tafsīr sessions delivered by qualified teachers — it is not Bazli's own knowledge, interpretation, or scholarly opinion. Every page credits its original source and speaker.

Where mistakes exist in the transcription, paraphrasing, or organization, they are the compiler's own, and not a reflection of the original teacher's words. Please refer back to the original lectures and to qualified scholars for anything that matters to your dīn.

اللَّهُمَّ انْفَعْنَا بِمَا عَلَّمْتَنَا وَعَلِّمْنَا مَا يَنْفَعُنَا
"O Allah, benefit us with what You have taught us, and teach us what will benefit us."

Hopefully beneficial

These notes were written first for the compiler's own benefit — a way to hold onto what was learned. If they end up being of any benefit to you as well, that is only by Allah's grace. Any good found here belongs to the original teachers; any shortcoming belongs to the compiler alone.
